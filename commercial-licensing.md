# Commercial licensing

LLMFetcher is available to commercial customers under a separately
negotiated, written license agreement.  This route is intended for customers
that need permissions not available under GPL-3.0, such as closed-source
deployment, internal redistribution, or a defined support commitment.

## What a commercial license can cover

Subject to the signed agreement, a commercial license may define:

- the licensed legal entity, permitted business purpose, territory, and term;
- deployment scope, including named environments, instances, users, or
  concurrency limits;
- whether modifications, contractor access, internal copying, sublicensing,
  or transfer are allowed;
- delivery version, acceptance criteria, installation, training, maintenance,
  service levels, and change-request rates;
- fees, renewal, termination, audit, confidentiality, liability, and warranty
  terms.

Commercial licenses are non-exclusive unless the agreement expressly says
otherwise.  An exclusive license should identify the exact field of use,
territory, term, and whether the exclusivity covers future versions.

## Boundaries of the license

The commercial license covers only the project code identified in the signed
agreement and only to the extent that the licensor owns the necessary rights.
It does not include:

- API accounts, model access, token charges, cloud infrastructure, or other
  third-party services;
- images, crawled pages, dataset records, knowledge-base content, club marks,
  player likenesses, or other third-party content in `pic/`, `kb/`, archives,
  or a customer's environment;
- third-party open-source components, which remain subject to their own
  notices and licenses.

Customer data should be supplied by the customer or accompanied by documented
authorization.  Production delivery should exclude local credentials,
runtime databases, private attachments, and unlicensed sample data unless the
agreement explicitly identifies them.

## Open-source alternative

The GPL-3.0-only edition remains available under [LICENSE](../LICENSE).  A
commercial agreement does not revoke GPL rights already granted to recipients
of the GPL edition.

## Contract checklist

The final contract should attach a versioned delivery inventory, a third-party
notice inventory, and an acceptance checklist.  It should expressly state
that the license is commercial (not a transfer of copyright), non-exclusive
or exclusive as applicable, and whether source code is delivered.  Have a
qualified lawyer review the agreement for the intended jurisdiction before
signing.
